✍️ 7 — How to Write OP Lv1 Without Boring — Scenes That Use Perfect at Lv1

Rival Heavy masters Destructive 45 — does 45 moves flashy but wobble edge. Master says: "You have the answer, not the handwriting." Body 100% shone, control 5% failed — readers love it.

Good OP Scene — Perfect Creates Choice, Not Win

Loved one poisoned. She has gold blood at Lv1 — could refine poison *inside* him by blood transfusion. But at 5% mastery, refine uncontrolled → might refine his medicine too. Choice: risk kill to save, or call herbalist like normal? Perfect enables *option* normal can't — tension, not auto-win.

Good OP Scene — Heaven's Eye Uses Perfect Against Her

She Unleashes at Lv15 vs bandit lord — wins beautiful 20s no Tax. Next day fever 3d (low mastery). During fever, rival assassin comes — she can't move. Perfect enabled win, *cost* enabled loss. Balance.

Rule: Lv1 Perfect wins *what* but loses *when*. She has tool for every problem at Lv1, but not wisdom *which* tool or *when* to stop. That's your 100-level story.
